# Honest Chess

Chess for Android phones, fully offline: play the computer at five honest
strength steps, or a friend on the same phone. No ads, no tracking, no
accounts, no purchases, and no Android permissions at all.

- [Privacy policy](privacy) — what the app collects, which is nothing.
